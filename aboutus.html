Snackabot

Snackabot is a vending machine business focused on reliable service, simple design, and modern automation.

We currently operate exclusively in Providence, Rhode Island, allowing us to stay hands-on, responsive, and consistent in how our machines are serviced and managed.

Our machines are intentionally designed to be straightforward and easy to use. Simple systems reduce failures, improve uptime, and make service faster when it matters.

Modern automation helps us monitor performance, restock efficiently, and respond quickly to issues—without overcomplicating the experience for customers or property owners.

We plan to target high-traffic business locations such as offices, apartment buildings, laundromats, and other commercial spaces where dependable vending service is expected.

Snackabot is scheduled to officially launch operations in 2027, with plans to expand into additional cities after establishing a strong operational foundation in Providence.
